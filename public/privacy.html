Privacy

What we store

  • Your Discord ID and display name, when you log in with Discord.
  • The Roblox username and details you enter when you place an order, together with the order status.

Cookies

One session cookie keeps you logged in, plus a short-lived one during the Discord login. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Where it lives

The site and its database run on Cloudflare. Order notifications are sent to our Discord server.

Questions

To ask about your data, contact the staff on our Discord server.
